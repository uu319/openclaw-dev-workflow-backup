## Telegram default outage + fix (2026-09-07 07:43-07:56 UTC)

**Lesson: once `channels.telegram.accounts` exists, the root `channels.telegram.botToken` is no
longer an active surface.** My SecretRef there was skipped with `[secrets] ...: secret ref is
configured on an inactive surface`, so `telegram:default` (main's bot) sat in a health-monitor
restart loop from 07:12, failing every 10 minutes with `UnresolvedSecretInputError`. The three
named accounts were unaffected because their refs sit on active account surfaces.

Fix: define `channels.telegram.accounts.default.botToken` with the same ref, then
`openclaw config unset channels.telegram.botToken`. `openclaw secrets reload` alone was not
enough — it re-resolves refs but does not pick up new config structure; a gateway restart was
required. Confirmed healthy after restart: default, developer, project-manager all
running/connected. <!-- project: path:/home/openclaw/.openclaw/workspace -->

When converting a channel token to a SecretRef, put the ref on the account surface, not the
channel root, and verify with `openclaw channels status` rather than trusting the write.
